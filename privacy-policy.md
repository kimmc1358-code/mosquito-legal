# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

JANIJU STUDIO ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Android mobile game **Catch Me If You Can: Mosquito** (the "Game"), package name **com.janiju.catchme.mosquito**.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game, including through Google services integrated into it. The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is distributed internationally.

## 1. Information Handled by the Game

### 1.1 Locally Stored Game Data

The Game stores the following information locally on your device:

- Your best survival time.
- Game settings, including sound-effect and background-music volume.
- Whether your advertisement-removal purchase has been verified.

We do not upload this local save file to a server operated by JANIJU STUDIO. Leaderboard submissions and purchase processing are handled separately through Google services, as described below. The Game does not provide cloud saves or use Google Play Games Saved Games.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK also maintains advertising consent information on your device.

### 1.2 Google Play Games Services

The Game uses **Google Play Games Services** for sign-in and a survival-time leaderboard. It attempts to sign in when the Game starts. If your Google Play Games account has already authorized the Game, sign-in may complete without an additional prompt. If automatic sign-in does not succeed, selecting the **Leaderboard** button may open Google's sign-in interface.

After a game ends, the Game submits your survival time, expressed in milliseconds, when you are signed in. A failed submission may be retried during a later session. If you are not signed in, your result is not submitted at that time; signing in later during the same session may submit your best result from that session.

Google processes account and player identifiers, profile information, and leaderboard data to provide these features. Leaderboard entries may display your Google Play Games player name, profile picture, and survival-time score to other players, depending on Google's service settings. The Game does not ask you to enter a separate name or email address and does not maintain its own profile database.

Google Play Games authentication may request Google account permissions associated with the integration. The Game does not use these permissions to provide cloud-save functionality.

### 1.3 Advertising

The Game uses **Google AdMob** to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, including:

- Advertising, app-set, device, or account identifiers, where applicable.
- Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location.
- App and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
- Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

The information processed depends on the SDK configuration, your device, region, and privacy choices. The Game does not request precise device location. Approximate location inferred from an IP address is different from access to your device's precise location.

Advertising partners identified in Google's consent interface may also process information in accordance with your choices and their own policies. Google's practices are described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 1.4 In-App Purchases

The Game offers an advertisement-removal purchase through **Google Play Billing**. Once the purchase is verified, all three advertisement types—banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements—are disabled.

Google processes payment and transaction information. We do not receive your full payment-card or banking details. The Game processes limited purchase information needed to verify your entitlement and restore the advertisement-removal feature, and stores the verified entitlement locally.

Removing advertisements does not delete information previously processed by Google or other advertising partners. It does not disable Google Play Games or purchase-related processing.

### 1.5 Contact and Feedback

The **Contact / Feedback** button opens your email application with a prepared message that may include the Game's version, device model, and Android version. You can review or edit the message before sending it. Opening this button does not automatically send information to us.

If you send an email, we receive your email address, the message contents, any included technical details, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and investigate reported issues. Your email provider also handles the message under its own policies.

### 1.6 Services and Permissions We Do Not Use

The Game does not provide separate account registration or nickname entry. It does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, a developer-operated game server, or a cloud-save service.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. It does not ask for your home address or phone number. Google SDKs may still process technical, advertising, purchase, and account information as described above.

## 2. Purposes of Processing

Information is handled to:

- Save your best survival time and preferences on your device.
- Authenticate your Google Play Games account and provide the leaderboard.
- Display advertisements and support advertising measurement and fraud prevention.
- Manage advertising consent and privacy choices.
- Verify and restore the advertisement-removal purchase.
- Respond to emails you choose to send and investigate issues.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell information that you provide directly to JANIJU STUDIO. Advertising-related collection and sharing through third-party SDKs is described separately in this Policy.

## 4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

The Game uses Google's UMP SDK to present advertising consent messages where applicable.

Where the Game makes privacy options available, you can open **Settings → Ad Privacy (EU)** to reopen Google's privacy options form and change your choices. This button is shown in applicable regions, such as European regions where advertising consent options are required, and is hidden elsewhere in the current Game.

Your choices may affect advertisement personalization or availability. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that occurred before withdrawal. Non-personalized or limited advertising may still involve some technical data processing.

You may also manage advertising identifiers and other privacy settings through Android settings and your Google account. Available controls depend on your Android version and the relevant Google service.

For privacy questions or requests where no in-game option is available, contact us using the address below. Information independently controlled by Google or an advertising partner must be managed through that provider's applicable controls or request process.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged **13 and older** and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

Minimum ages and parental-consent requirements may differ by country. Users must satisfy applicable age requirements for the Game and the third-party services they use.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact **janiju.studio@gmail.com**. We will review the request and take appropriate steps to delete information under our control where required. Information held by Google or another provider is subject to that provider's policies and controls.

## 6. Retention

Local game data generally remains on your device until you clear the Game's application data or uninstall it. Device or platform backup settings may affect whether local data is restored after reinstallation.

If you email us, we retain the correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve the issue, and satisfy applicable legal obligations. We do not maintain a separate server-side gameplay or player-profile database.

Google and other third-party providers retain information they process according to their own policies and legal obligations. Removing the Game or clearing its local data does not automatically delete leaderboard entries, purchase records, or advertising information held by these providers.

## 7. Deletion and Account Controls

You can remove local game data by clearing the Game's application data in Android settings or uninstalling the Game.

You can manage or delete Google Play Games information through your Google Play Games profile and Google's available controls. See [Delete your Play Games profile and data](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646). Google Play purchase records and other Google information are managed through the relevant Google service and may be retained where legally required.

The Game has no separately registered JANIJU STUDIO account to delete.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information we directly hold, such as email correspondence, contact **janiju.studio@gmail.com**. We may request limited information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. Some information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations.
